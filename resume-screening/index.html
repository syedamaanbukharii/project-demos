AI Resume Screening System — Interactive Demo

Explainable ranking: every sub-score carries evidence from the resume. Edit anything below and re-run.

About this demo: it runs entirely in your browser with fictional candidates. In the real system an LLM parses PDF/DOCX resumes and embeddings live in PostgreSQL + pgvector; here a rule-based parser and TF-IDF similarity stand in for those steps. The five-factor scoring, weighting, ranking and evidence trail are the real logic — change the job description, resumes or weights and the ranking updates deterministically.
