Radiology Drafting Assistant — Interactive Demo

Drafts a report from imaging notes, then uses retrieval over reporting guidelines to flag statements the notes do not support.

Research prototype · fictional data · not medical advice. Nothing here should be used for clinical decisions. The guideline snippets are short paraphrased reporting conventions written for this demo.
About this demo: runs in your browser. In the real prototype an LLM writes the draft; here a rule-based extractor + template writer stand in for it. The verification logic is real: findings are extracted with laterality, negation and size; every draft sentence is checked against them (supported / unsupported / contradicted / needs hedging); and BM25 retrieval over a guideline corpus attaches the most relevant citation.
