Streaming & batch inference, a RAG pipeline and multi-agent workflows behind one dashboard.

About this demo: it runs in your browser with a simulated model (no API calls, nothing leaves your machine). The platform mechanics are real code: token streaming with cancel and latency metrics, a concurrency-limited batch queue with retries and exponential backoff, sentence-window chunking + BM25 retrieval with a groundedness threshold, and a multi-agent graph (Planner → Researcher → Writer → Critic) with a conditional revision loop.
